## Przemek Sekalski

**PhD, Eng.** — Director of the IT Technology Centre at **Lodz University of Technology**, lecturer in the Department of Microelectronics and Computer Science, and founder of several companies built with students.

Formally *Przemysław Sękalski*. Everyone calls me Przemek.

---

### What I work on

**System architecture** across products that are live and have users — Speakersi, Spikacz, Lark AI Edu and others. The job is deciding where the boundaries go: what runs where, what talks to what, which part is allowed to be clever and which part has to be boring and reliable.

**Before that, mobile** — iTaxi, Booksy, GeckonCam and others, when mobile was the hard problem.

**And the research half** — a master's on a micromachined pressure sensor at LAAS-CNRS, a doctorate at DESY, then work with CERN, Cornell, INFN and CEA–IN2P3. Embedded systems, IoT, applied machine learning, and patents on data and video processing.

Most of what I build is not open source, so this profile is not where the work is. It is where I can be reached about code.

---

### Students

I have supervised close to **300 diploma projects**, and the rule for every one of them is the same: it has to end in something that runs. Some did not stop there — **Open-RnD**, **Smart Soft Solutions**, **Simple Machine Learning**, **myHydro**, **EcoCPR** and **LARK AI Solutions** were founded with students.

**Add me to your repository** if you want me to look at your code rather than talk about it. A link to a specific line beats a paragraph describing it, and it costs you ten seconds.

---

### Teaching

I teach a course and a four-hour workshop on building with AI agents — how the loop works, why a written specification outlives a chat log, and what happens when an agent reads a file somebody else wrote. Materials are shared with participants each run.
